1. eigenvector
- a.k.a. characteristic vector
- vectors that are only stretched/shrunk without rotation or shear for a given linear transformation
- eigenvector v of linear transformation T is scaled by constant factor λ when linear transformation is applied to it
Tv=λv

2. eigenvalue
- a.k.a. characteristic value
- multiplying factor λ from linear transformation of eigenvector
