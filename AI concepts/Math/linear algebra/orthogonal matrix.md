1. orthogonal matrix
- real square matrix
- columns and rows are orthonormal vectors
- characteristics
   - Q^T^Q = QQ^T^ = I
   - necessarily invertible; Q^T^ = Q^-1^
   - unitary; Q^-1^ = Q*
   - normal over real numbers; Q*Q = QQ*
