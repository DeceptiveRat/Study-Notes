1. matrix
- matrix: no parenthesis
- **p**matrix: parenthesis ()
- **b**matrix: brackets []
- **B**matrix: curly braces {}
- **v**matrix: vertical bar brackets ||
- **V**matrix: double vertical bar brackets || ||

2. elements
- &: columns
- \: rows

3. large matrix
- \cdots: horizontal 3 dots
- \vdots: vertical 3 dots
- \ddots: across 3 dots
