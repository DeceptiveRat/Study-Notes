1. Bayes rule

```latex
\documentclass{article}
\pagestyle{empty}
\usepackage{amsmath}
\begin{document}
\begin{align*}
P(Y|X) = \frac{P(X|Y)P(Y)}{P(X)}
\end{align*}
\end{document}
```

- P(Y|X): posterior probability of Y
- P(Y): prior probability of Y
- P(X|Y): likelihood probability of X
- P(X): evidence probability of X
