## 1. definition
- social engineering attack
- text-based messaging used
- sensitive information is stolen

## 2. how
- sense of urgency; motivation for the victim
- realistic email address
- realistic website

## 3. types
### 3.1. spear phishing
- target specific individuals; name, title, etc

### 3.2. whaling
- targets executives

### 3.3. business email compromise (BEC)
- impersonates senior executives

### 3.4. clone phishing
- replica of an authentic email is used

### 3.5. vishing
- impersonates official; intimidation

### 3.6. snowshoeing
- low volume of messages from multiple domains and IPs; circumvents spam filter

## 4. identification
- verify address
- grammar and spelling
