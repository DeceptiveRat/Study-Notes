## 1. What are botnets?
a group of malware-infected and internet-connected bots controlled by a threat actor. Most botnets have C&C servers

## 2. Botnet topologies
- star topology: organized around a central server
- multi-server topology: multiple C&C servers
- hierarchical topology: multiple C&C servers in tiered groups
- random topology
- P2P: each bot operates independently as both server and client. Harder to detect

## 3. Botnet uses
- distribute malware
- gather information
- DDoS
